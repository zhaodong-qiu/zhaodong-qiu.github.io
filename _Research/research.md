I prefer to portray myself as an observer who possesses an acute sensitivity to interesting events and interactions within the organizational context, continually striving to uncover their underlying patterns and logic. Throughout my academic journey, my daily experiences have not only served as an observatory for emerging questions but also as a constant reminder of the profound interplay between individual agency and the broader systems at play. As individuals operate as integral units within a larger system, their agency is both influenced by the surrounding environment and, in turn, impacts it. Derived from these observations, my two primary research streams converge to illuminate the intricate dynamics between individual agency and the systemic environments in which employees are deeply embedded.
