Zhaodong (Alan) Qiu is a doctoral candidate in Leadership and Organization Management at the School of Economics and Management at Tsinghua University.

Zhaodong's research investigates the intricate interaction between individual agency and the systems in which employees are embedded in. He navigates his research questions through various quantitative methods like experience sampling method, field experiment, and longitudinal survey. His research has been published in leading journals such as the *Journal of Applied Psychology*.
